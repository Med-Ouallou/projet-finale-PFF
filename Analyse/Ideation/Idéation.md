## Ideation – Solutions per Actor

Based on the empathy analysis, the following solutions were proposed for each main actor to address their specific needs and pain points.

---

### 1. Restaurant Owner (Admin)
**Solutions:**
- Centralized admin dashboard for overall restaurant management
- Automated revenue calculation and daily/monthly reports
- Staff performance monitoring and role management
- Real-time statistics and visual charts for quick insights
- Notifications for pending tasks and orders

---

### 2. Staff / Employees
**Solutions:**
- Real-time order management interface
- Task organization system for staff assignments
- Alerts and notifications for new orders

---

### 3. Customers
**Solutions:**
- Online menu with updated availability status
- Table reservation system
- Online order placement and tracking
- Digital payment options
- Quick and organized service with confirmation notifications

### Global System Features
- Role-Based Access Control (Admin / Staff / Customer)
- Secure authentication system
- Responsive and mobile-friendly web interface
- Optional features: PDF invoices, inventory management, and notifications
