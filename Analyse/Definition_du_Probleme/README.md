# Définition du Problème

Small restaurant owners often rely on manual processes to manage daily operations such as order taking, revenue tracking, menu updates, and staff coordination.

This manual management leads to:  
- Order confusion during peak hours  
- Revenue calculation errors  
- Lack of real-time performance visibility  
- Poor organization of menu availability  
- Increased operational stress  

Despite having strong culinary expertise, the restaurant owner struggles with workflow organization and system control.

The absence of a centralized digital management system limits efficiency, scalability, and overall business performance.
