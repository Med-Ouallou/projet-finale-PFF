# Carte d’Empathie

## The Persona
**Who is he?**  
Owner of a small restaurant, passionate about food and customer service.  
Skilled in kitchen operations but struggling with organization and daily management.  
His challenge is not cooking — it’s system control and workflow management.

---

## Goal
**What does he want?**
- Organize orders efficiently  
- Track revenue automatically  
- Reduce stress during peak hours  
- Improve overall restaurant performance  

He wants a simple and reliable management system.

---

## What He Sees
- Competitors using digital systems  
- Customers expecting fast service  
- Confusion during busy hours  

He understands that digital organization is necessary.

---

## What He Says
- “I need better order management.”  
- “Manual tracking is no longer efficient.”  
- “I want clear revenue visibility.”  

He seeks structure and control.

---

## What He Does
- Manages orders manually  
- Calculates totals by hand  
- Tracks revenue in notebooks  
- Coordinates staff verbally  

His workflow is manual and not scalable.

---

## What He Hears
- Customers asking about order status  
- Advice to digitalize the restaurant  
- Market moving toward automation  

External pressure pushes him to modernize.

---

## Think & Feel

### Pains
- Order confusion  
- Revenue tracking errors  
- Operational stress  

### Gains
- Automated workflows  
- Clear dashboard insights  
- Better organization and control
